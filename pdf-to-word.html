JNR PDF to Text · Help

Native extraction, scanned documents and image-aware exports.

1. Upload your PDF. Drag it onto the dotted area or choose it from your device. Processing happens in the browser; the PDF is not sent to a JNR server.

2. Choose the best mode. Smart auto-detect uses selectable PDF text first and OCRs pages that contain little or no selectable text. “OCR every page” can help with scanned pages that also contain selectable labels. “Embedded text only” skips OCR.

3. Pick the OCR language. OCR supports many printed scripts, but the relevant language pack is downloaded on first use. Large or multiple language packs can take time and memory. In Smart mode, detected embedded figures can be OCRed separately; recognised words appear below the image marker.

4. Download your result. TXT contains editable text, page labels and [IMAGE] markers near detected image positions. HTML + images creates a self-contained HTML document with the original rendered page image and extracted text. Original page snapshots preserve exact visual layout. Plain TXT cannot embed images.

Accuracy and privacy

PDFs with embedded text usually extract more accurately than OCR. No OCR system can promise zero mistakes on every scan or font, especially with handwriting, blurred pages, unusual symbols, tables or mixed scripts. Check important details after extraction. Browser memory and device speed limit how many high-resolution page images can be processed at once.

Troubleshooting

If OCR libraries or language data do not load, check your internet connection and reload the page. Password-protected PDFs may need to be unlocked first. For very long PDFs, process page ranges such as 1-20 or 21-40.
