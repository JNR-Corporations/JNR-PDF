Important: Visual rebuild is a rescue method, not a lossless repair. It turns pages into images, so searchable/selectable text, hyperlinks, annotations, forms, bookmarks, accessibility structure, layers and digital signatures are not preserved. The tool cannot reconstruct missing pixels or content that no parser can read. Keep the original file as a backup.
