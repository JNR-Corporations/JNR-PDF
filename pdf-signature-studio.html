This tool adds a visible signature image (electronic mark) to the selected PDF pages. It is not a certificate-based digital signature and does not cryptographically lock or authenticate a document. PDF forms, annotations, bookmarks or signatures already present may be affected by PDF rewriting. Password-protected files may not open. Always check the exported file.
