Mixed formats

OOXML presentation families and OpenDocument presentation files use format-aware paths where possible.

No silent skips

File package and slide counts are checked. Conversion warns or stops if it cannot reliably identify the complete deck.

Format compatibility
Know what can be rendered in this browser tool before converting.
Format familyStatusNotes
.pptx · .pptmSupported pathOOXML presentation packages; macro content is not executed.
.ppsx · .ppsmSupported pathOOXML slide-show variants with presentation parts; rendering quality depends on deck features.
.potx · .potmSupported pathOOXML template variants if they contain standard presentation parts.
.odp · .otp · .fodpSupported pathOpenDocument presentations; renderer preserves positioned text, basic fills, frames and embedded images. Complex effects may simplify.
.ppt · .pps · .potNeeds conversion firstLegacy binary formats are not safely parsed by this browser renderer. Open in PowerPoint/LibreOffice and save as PPTX or export directly to PDF.
.sldx · .sldmMay not be a full deckSlide-library items do not necessarily contain a complete presentation. The tool validates for a standard presentation root before processing.
.key · .ppa · .ppamUnsupported hereKeynote files and PowerPoint add-ins are proprietary/add-in containers rather than standard, fully renderable presentation decks.
