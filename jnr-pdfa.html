This tool rasterizes pages in the browser and creates a new PDF with PDF/A identification XMP and an embedded sRGB output intent. It is designed as a browser-only best-effort archival candidate, not a substitute for formal veraPDF validation. Complex, very large, encrypted or unsupported PDFs may fail to render. Keep the original unchanged.
